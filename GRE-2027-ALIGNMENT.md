## Coverage and response behavior

Preparation covers text completion with one to three blanks, sentence equivalence, and reading comprehension. Reading requires single-answer, multiple-answer and sentence-selection interactions. ETS describes passage questions as approximately half the Verbal measure; this is not a fixed per-section quota. Multi-blank and multi-answer responses receive credit only when complete and correct. Sentence equivalence requires two answers that produce sentences with equivalent meanings. [ETS Verbal overview](https://www.ets.org/gre/test-takers/general-test/prepare/content/verbal-reasoning.html)

The interface presents each text-completion blank independently. Reading sentence selection uses whole passage sentences. Review explains the evidence, the tempting wrong answer and a reusable strategy. These learning interactions do not claim to replicate the exact ETS interface or question distribution.

## Scoring and a 170 Verbal goal

Verbal uses a 130–170 scale. The difficulty of the second Verbal section depends on first-section performance, and ETS equates scores across difficulty differences. Original app difficulty labels, percentage correct and XP do not establish an ETS-equivalent score. App simulations do not reproduce official adaptive routing. [ETS scoring explanation](https://www.ets.org/gre/test-takers/general-test/scores/understand-scores.html)

The preparation sequence in `targetScorePlan` is Wordtrail's recommendation: diagnose, repair vocabulary and reasoning gaps, review errors and lucky guesses, test transfer to unfamiliar questions, practice pacing, then benchmark with fresh official material. It supports an ambitious goal without guaranteeing 170. No universal “allowed mistakes for 170” threshold is asserted.

There is no deduction for an incorrect response. Use remaining time to answer rather than leave blanks. Mark-and-return behavior is meaningful only within the current section. [ETS test-day tips, page 20](https://www.ets.org/pdfs/gre/getting-into-business-school.pdf)
